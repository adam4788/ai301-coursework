# Rubric: is this pull request ready to submit?

## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| plan-fidelity | Entire diff and commit changes against Plan context or plan.md, including deviations; description against those changes. | Every substantive change is in the accepted plan or explicitly recorded as a justified issue-related deviation, and every omitted plan item is openly deferred in the plan/deviations and outgoing description. Supporting tests/docs count within named folders or purposes. No unrelated cleanup or hidden expansion; no false claim of exact adherence. | required |
| observable-tests | Test evidence against the plan's repro, targeted checks, relevant controls, and applicable repo-required commands. | The package supplies reproducible actions or commands and concrete observed results demonstrating the changed behavior, including the plan's relevant controls and applicable planned/repository checks. Actual observations may be prose, command annotations, or printed output; do not demand a raw transcript for every suite/control when its execution and outcome are explicitly reported alongside a concrete issue reproduction. A specific named command with its reported result counts; generic “tests pass” alone, expected-only results, placeholders, or testing a different case do not. A failing or unavailable check may pass when its actual outcome, reason and remaining limitation are honestly disclosed, not replaced by a success claim. | required |
| reviewable-diff | All diff hunks and commits against the issue-related boundary; explanatory PR text. | A reviewer can isolate the focused fix and supporting changes without unrelated edits, debug output, abandoned commented-out code, accidental files or unexplained mechanical churn. Necessary broad changes have an issue-related reason; size or commit count alone never fails. | required |
| faithful-claims | Title, description and checked boxes against the full diff, test evidence and recorded deviations. | Every material claim is supported or explicitly qualified. Concrete reported execution and observations in Test evidence support claims even without a raw transcript; compare them with the supplied diff and plan, not assumptions about an unseen codebase. Added fixtures alone are not represented as executed tests; skipped checks, failing outcomes, known limits and scoped-down work are disclosed rather than hidden. No contradicted or genuinely unevidenced fixed/passed/unchanged claim. | required |
| stated-standards | Repo facts, issue/thread requirements and policy compared with PR title, description and supporting artifacts. | Every applicable stated PR-template and contribution ask is met, including issue reference, required sections/checklists, changelog or docs where required, and truthful AI-use disclosure where required. An explicit justified N/A can satisfy a nonapplicable section. Do not invent a template, AI rule, successful CI requirement or other obligation when none is stated. In live Path Review mode require course AI disclosure in Notes for Reviewers; pre-opening CI may remain unticked. | required |
| clear-summary | Title and description against the issue and diff. | Plain, specific wording lets a reviewer identify the change and its purpose without generic praise or unsupported promises; terse complete writing is sufficient. | preferred |

## Verdict rule

Accept only if every required check is pass. Any required fail or unclear means reject; an unverifiable required claim does not pass. Preferred fail or unclear is advisory and never changes the verdict. Complete all rows even after a blocker. An honestly disclosed shortfall is not itself rejection, but disclosure does not excuse unrelated edits, fabrication, or ignoring an applicable mandatory repository ask.
