hey everyone, I built Meeting Copilot, an AI assisted meeting plugin for Hermes. Um, it's a community colla-catalog submission and was approved and merged after maintainer review. Thank you um, nous-research team. Um, the idea came from a familiar problem, being in a meeting and struggling to recall work or my teammates what my teammates had already done. Uh, use AI to help implement the plugin while I directed its features, usability, visual design, and um others. One of the most valuable improvements came from review. Audio worker inherited more environment setting than it needed, potentially exposing credentials. The fix restricted what the worker could receive, disabled automatic Hugging Face token use, and added regression test. The maintainer reviewed and the changes got approved. Um There's still work ahead, especially making the setup simpler and audio behavior more consistent across Mac and Windows. My goal is straightforward, install the plugin and start using it with far less configuration. Building it has reminded me that useful feature is only part of the job. Clear boundaries, review, testing, and everyday usability matter just as much.
